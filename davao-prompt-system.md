## 4. AI Audit & Literature Verification Framework

### Goal
Use AI engines to generate comprehensive literature reviews on Mindanao development priorities, then conduct manual human audit to catch, flag, and correct informational fabrications or biased assessments.

### Role
Academic Research Associate working for a university research center or regional policy think tank in Davao.

### Audience
Research Director demanding absolute structural integrity and empirical verification before policy briefs proceed.

### Situation
Research team running on tight deadlines needs to synthesize local economic papers. AI can summarize massive text blocks in seconds, but risk of unverified statistics or false citations undermines institutional credibility.

### Deliverable
**Vetted Literature Matrix & Critique Log** — Automated summaries mapped against confirmed primary sources, published to GitHub portfolio.

#### 2. Critical Reflection on Tool Limitations

**Key Insights:**
- AI platforms can consolidate dense policy documents into clear thematic summaries in minutes
- However, AI frequently conflates aspirational targets with current capacity measurements
- Numbers from outdated sources (>2 years old) are prone to hallucination
- Sector-specific jargon (e.g., "baseload power," "grid penetration") requires subject-matter expert validation

**Audit Best Practices:**
1. Cross-reference all quantitative claims against official government statistics portals
2. Flag any citation without a publication year or institutional source
3. When AI provides multiple statistics in one statement, verify each independently
4. Document the original AI prompt used to generate the claim (for reproducibility)
